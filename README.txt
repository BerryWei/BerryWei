I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the AI for Engineering Applications division, Department of Civil Engineering at National Taiwan University, where I completed my Ph.D. in 2026. My research applies Artificial Intelligence (AI) to materials science and engineering, focusing on deep material networks and physics-informed surrogate models for multiscale homogenization. Broadly, my work aims to bridge the gap between the microstructural characteristics of materials and their macroscopic mechanical properties through the design of innovative computational models — spanning polycrystalline plasticity, texture evolution, multiphysics coupling, and microstructure foundation models.
